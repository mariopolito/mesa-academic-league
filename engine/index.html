What should the engine call you?

It goes on your engine's nameplate.

The engine uses the name you gave the study site. To change it, click the line under MESA ACADEMIC LEAGUE on the study site; the engine picks it up the next time you come back.

Keys: 1–4 answer · H hint · Enter skips the wait
How the engine works

Answer questions to earn sparks. Harder questions earn more, and every answer also pays out ten seconds of what your gears make.

The pressure gauge multiplies every answer. Each right answer adds pressure, up to ×3. A miss vents one zone, so a full gauge takes four misses to empty.

Spend sparks on gears. Each gear is a topic, and it makes sparks by itself. While you are away it keeps going at a tenth of its speed, for up to 8 hours, and when you come back after two minutes or more a report shows what your gears did. While you study on the study site (flashcards, matching, quizzes, anything), your gears stop rusting and run at half speed instead.

Gears rust. An unused gear slowly loses its shine, and a rusty gear makes less. Answer that topic's questions right to polish it. Mastery makes a gear stronger and makes it rust more slowly, so what you really know keeps paying.

Upgrades cost sparks, but each one also asks you to prove something first.

A right answer moves on by itself. Miss one and the right answer lights up: tap it to go on.

Tools turn up as you play: after a right answer now and then, every 10 in a row, with golden prizes and with achievements. 50/50 and Cross out take away wrong answers, Second chance waits until you pick a wrong one, Swap trades the question without breaking your streak, and Double up doubles your next 5 answers. A question answered with a hint, 50/50, Cross out or Second chance still pays, but does not count toward mastering it. You can hold 9 of each tool at first, and the toolbox upgrades raise that to 99. A tool earned when its slot is full is sold for sparks. From level 21, each gear level also costs one of every tool (two of each from level 31, and so on), so you need all five kinds. The Tool shop upgrade lets you buy and sell tools, and the Trading post lets you swap the ones you have plenty of for the ones you are short of.

Hints unlock 5 seconds into a question.

Golden questions turn up about once in every 50, in any topic. They are the tip-offs. Get one right to win a jackpot of sparks or 60 seconds of overdrive, when every gear makes 5×.
